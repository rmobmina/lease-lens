          About LeaseLens

          A RentSafeTO score sums up a whole inspection in one number. LeaseLens keeps the number and shows what sits underneath it: where it is, what was inspected, and how it has changed.

          LeaseLens is an independent student-built project using public City of Toronto data. It is not an official City of Toronto service.

          Analysts: the data explorer shows every record, every filter and full data notes.

          How to use it

          1. Compare areas. Start with the city map and zoom into a ward to see where lower scores cluster. Explore the map
          2. Check one building. Select it and inspect its results, category by category.
          3. Look at its history. A score describes inspection day. See whether it has been rising or falling. Re-inspection changes

          Reading the scores

          Scores describe common areas on the inspection day, not individual units, rents or landlords. Patterns here are associations; they don’t show what causes a low score.

          The City changed its scoring system in 2023. The two systems use different items and scales, so they are never compared with each other here.

          Methods and limits

          How ratings are counted

          Since 2023, inspectors rate up to 50 items from 0 to 3, where 3 is the top mark. N/A means an item doesn’t apply. Neither N/A nor a blank is ever counted as a pass or as a problem. “Lowest 10%” and “lowest 25%” are citywide cut-offs on each building’s latest score in the chosen system, with ties included.

          What is real and what is drawn
          On the pageWhat it is
          Scores, dates, item ratingsActual records from the City’s Apartment Building Evaluations files.
          Building shape and height (“massing” badge)Actual footprint and height from the City’s 3D Massing data, used only when the address matched one footprint reliably.
          Building shape (“approximate” badge)Estimated from the registered number of storeys. Not the real shape.
          X-Ray interiors, entrances, parking and site detailsConceptual. They show where a category belongs, not a real floor plan.
          Homepage drawing of downtownActual City footprints, heights, streets and shoreline, simplified: small buildings and minor streets are left out.
          What we will not do
          • Invent scores, dates, ratings or comparisons. Every number is computed from the City’s published records.
          • Mix the two scoring systems.
          • Treat a missing or N/A rating as a pass. Missing results stay missing.
          • Present the X-Ray’s interior as a real floor plan, or suggest a problem is in a particular apartment.
          Colours

          Blue, white and red, inspired by the Toronto flag (Pantone 287 and 186; the screen colours are approximations). Red marks low scores and declines only, and charts never rely on red and blue alone.

          Help

          Exploring the map

          The map starts with Toronto’s 25 wards. Zoom in (scroll, pinch, the + and − buttons, or + and − on the keyboard while the map has focus) and nearby buildings appear as groups, then as individual buildings. Select a ward or a building to open its details beside the map; × or Esc closes them without moving the map.

          Finding a building

          Type an address in the search box at the top, move through the matches with ↑ and ↓, and press Enter. The map flies to the building. Each ward card and the “Buildings in view” list are also keyboard routes to individual buildings.

          Going deeper

          From a building, Inspect building opens its X-Ray, Compare nearby lists the closest evaluated buildings, and View history shows every evaluation. Back to map returns to the same place and zoom. The browser’s Back and Forward buttons, and copied links, work at every level.

          Layers and analysis

          Layers holds the scoring system, which buildings are shown, and the ward. Analysis opens one chart at a time beside the map, for the selected ward or all of Toronto. The two scoring systems are never mixed.

          The guided story

          Use Next and Back, or the ← and → keys. You can leave the story at any time; your previous view and filters come back as they were.

          Building X-Ray

          Drag to orbit, right-drag or use two fingers to pan, scroll to zoom, and double-click to fly to a point. Select a category in the list or on the model to open its items. Esc returns to the whole building. The camera stays outside the building.

          Information buttons

          Buttons marked i open a short explanation. Press Esc or select outside it to close.
