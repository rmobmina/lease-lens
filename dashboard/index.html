Inspection fingerprint

What does a 92 actually hide?

A RentSafeTO score folds dozens of inspection items into one number. , so the score alone says little about what went wrong. Lay the items out side by side and two buildings with the same score can look nothing alike.

Don't just ask what a building scored. Ask what it scored low on.

The fingerprint uses the item ratings from the City's current scoring system (2023 – now). The 2017 – 2023 system rated different items on a different scale.

Reading these charts

Scores describe the condition of common areas on the inspection day, not individual units, rents or landlord practices. Patterns here are associations; they don't show what causes a low score.

The City changed its scoring system in 2023. Scores from the two systems use different rating scales and items, so they're never compared with each other here.

Item ratings

Since 2023, inspectors rate up to 50 items from 0 to 3, where 3 is the top mark. N/A means an item doesn't apply and is never counted as a problem. The inspection fingerprint explains how the ratings are grouped and counted.
