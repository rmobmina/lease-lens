              About LeaseLens

              A score is a starting point.

              A RentSafeTO score sums up a building inspection in one number. LeaseLens shows what sits underneath it: where the building is, what inspectors rated, and how its score has changed.

              Data & methodology

              Understanding the scores
              • Scores describe a building’s common areas on the inspection day, not individual units, rents or landlords.
              • A high score does not prove a building is safe; a low one does not prove it is unsafe.
              • Since 2023, inspectors rate up to 50 items from 0 to 3 (3 is the top mark). LeaseLens calls 0 or 1 “rated low”.
              • N/A means an item doesn’t apply. Neither N/A nor a blank is ever counted as a pass or a problem.
              • The City changed its scoring system in 2023. The two rate different items on different scales, so they are never compared or connected.
              • “Lowest 10%” and “lowest 25%” are citywide cut-offs on each building’s latest score in one system, ties included.

              Limitations
              • Patterns here are associations. They don’t show what causes a low score.
              • Missing or N/A ratings stay missing; they are never treated as passes.
              • Building interiors are illustrative. They never place a problem in a particular apartment.
              • Every number is computed from the City’s published records. Nothing is estimated, adjusted or invented.

              Help

              The keys and controls, one place at a time. Back and Forward, and copied links, work everywhere.

              The map

              Starts with Toronto’s 25 wards. Zoom in (scroll, pinch, + and −) to see groups, then single buildings. Select a ward or building to open its details; × or Esc closes them.

              Finding a building

              Type an address in the search box, move through matches with ↑ and ↓, and press Enter. The map flies to it. Ward cards and “Buildings in view” are keyboard routes too.

              Layers and analysis

              Layers sets the scoring system, which buildings show and the ward. Analysis opens one chart at a time beside the map. The two scoring systems are never mixed.

              A building

              Inspection: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, select a category to see what inspectors found. Compare: a building with the same or closest score. History: every evaluation. Esc steps back one level.

              The guided story

              Use Next and Back, or the ← and → keys. Leave at any time; your previous view and filters come back as they were.

              Information buttons

              Buttons marked i open a short explanation. Methodology in a building explains how its views are made. Esc or a click outside closes them.
